# E-Commerce Sales, Profitability & Customer Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes an e-commerce sales dataset to understand sales performance, profitability, customer segments, regional performance, and shipping operations.

The analysis was built in Power BI using Power Query for data preparation, a star-schema data model, and DAX measures for business KPIs.

The aim was to turn raw transaction data into practical business insights that can support decisions around products, customers, regions, profitability, and operations.

## Key KPIs

- Total Sales: approximately 13M
- Total Profit: approximately 1.47M
- Profit Margin: approximately 11.6%
- Total Orders: approximately 25K
- Total Customers: approximately 5K
- Average Order Value: approximately 505

## Key Business Insights

### 1. Technology is the strongest category

Technology generated the highest sales at approximately 4.75M and also produced the highest profit at approximately 664K.

This makes Technology the strongest overall category based on both revenue and profitability.

### 2. Furniture needs closer profitability attention

Furniture generated approximately 4.11M in sales, but its profit was only around 285K.

This shows that strong sales volume does not automatically translate into strong profitability. Furniture is therefore an area where pricing, discounting, and product-level performance should be reviewed.

### 3. Smartphones are important top-selling products

The Top 10 Products analysis shows several smartphone products among the highest-selling products, including Apple, Cisco, Motorola, and Nokia smartphone products.

These products are important contributors to sales and could be monitored for inventory, pricing, and future sales planning.

### 4. Consumer is the strongest customer segment

Consumer customers contribute the highest sales and profit among the three segments.

Approximate profit contribution:
- Consumer: 650K
- Corporate: 382K
- Home Office: 231K

This makes Consumer the most important segment to retain and grow, while also providing an opportunity to understand the lower contribution from Corporate and Home Office.

### 5. Regional performance is uneven

Central is the highest-sales region in the dashboard, while several other regions contribute considerably less.

This suggests that regional performance should not be treated uniformly. High-performing regions can be studied for successful practices, while lower-performing regions may need targeted sales or customer strategies.

### 6. Standard Class dominates shipping volume

Standard Class accounts for approximately 15.2K orders, considerably more than the other shipping modes.

It also has the highest average delivery time at approximately 5 days.

This highlights an operational trade-off: Standard Class is the most commonly used option, but it also has the longest average delivery time.

### 7. Discounting and profitability should be monitored

The Discount vs Profit analysis shows that profitability varies across sub-categories at different discount levels.

This chart identifies areas for further investigation, but it should not be interpreted as proof that discounts directly cause lower profit. Product mix, pricing, quantity, and regional demand can also influence profitability.

## Recommendations

### Improve Furniture profitability

Review low-profit furniture products, discount levels, and pricing strategies. The objective should be to improve profit without sacrificing the category's significant sales volume.

### Protect the Technology category

Technology is the strongest category by both sales and profit. High-performing products should receive appropriate inventory planning and continued attention to customer demand.

### Focus on the Consumer segment

Since Consumer customers contribute the most sales and profit, retention campaigns and targeted offers could help increase customer value.

### Review regional differences

Compare high-performing regions with weaker regions to identify differences in product demand, customer mix, pricing, and profitability.

### Evaluate shipping strategy

Standard Class is the dominant shipping mode but also has the longest average delivery time. The business could evaluate whether selected orders or customers would benefit from faster shipping options without unnecessarily increasing shipping costs.

### Use discounts carefully

Discount decisions should consider profitability rather than sales alone. High sales with weak profit can hide an underlying margin problem.

## Conclusion

The analysis shows that business performance is not evenly distributed across categories, products, customer segments, regions, and shipping methods.

Technology is the strongest category, Consumer is the strongest customer segment, and Standard Class dominates shipping volume. At the same time, Furniture presents a clear profitability opportunity, while regional and discount-level differences deserve further investigation.

The dashboard moves the analysis beyond simply reporting sales numbers and helps identify where the business is performing well, where profitability can improve, and which operational areas may need attention.
